Apex Digital Labs Private Limited pledges a total bonus pool of 200000 rupees for four application developers: Priya Nair, Karan Kapoor, Divya Joshi, and Arjun Mehta. Apex Digital Labs Private Limited will deposit 200000 rupees into the escrow vault. The bonus is paid across two milestones. In the first milestone, each of the four developers receives 25000 rupees when the test lead signs the test report. In the second milestone, each of the four developers receives 25000 rupees on the product launch date of 15 November 2026. This creates eight payouts of 25000 rupees each. Sunil Chawla, who chairs the staff committee and is not an engineering team member, will act as the independent judge to certify milestones and resolve disputes. All payments will be released directly to the respective developers upon milestone verification.
