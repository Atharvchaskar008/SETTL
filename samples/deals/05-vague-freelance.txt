Meera Kapoor agrees to hire freelance designer Rahul Verma to create a branding package and website design for about 20000 rupees. The agreement specifies that Meera Kapoor will deposit the payment into the escrow vault before design work starts. Rahul Verma will deliver the complete design concepts within a reasonable time following the kickoff discussion. Payment will be released to Rahul Verma when the work is satisfactory according to the client. In case of any dispute or disagreement regarding the quality or schedule, Vikram Joshi will act as the independent judge to review the situation and make a binding determination. Both parties agree to communicate progress weekly through email until project completion.
