Vikram Malhotra agrees to hire freelance web developer Ananya Sen to build an online portfolio website for a total price of 30000 rupees. Vikram Malhotra will deposit the full amount of 30000 rupees into the escrow vault before work begins. Payment is split into three milestones. The first stage of 10000 rupees for design is released once the client receives the design files for five pages. The second stage of 15000 rupees for development is released when all web pages are deployed to the staging server and working. The final stage of 5000 rupees for launch is released once the website is deployed to the production domain and accessible publicly. If any disagreement arises between the parties, Siddharth Rao will act as the neutral judge to review the deliverables and resolve the issue. Ananya Sen will deliver all work according to these milestones.
