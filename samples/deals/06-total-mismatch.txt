Pooja Singhania agrees to engage building contractor Amit Chawla for renovation work on her duplex home. The stated agreement total is 250000 rupees, with 120000 rupees for structure framing and 150000 rupees for roof finishing. Pooja Singhania will deposit the stated total of 250000 rupees into the escrow vault before construction begins. In the first stage, Amit Chawla receives 120000 rupees when the steel framing structure is erected and inspected. In the second stage, Amit Chawla receives 150000 rupees when the waterproof roof slab casting is completed and cured. Neha Kulkarni, a licensed structural engineer, will act as the independent judge to inspect the construction milestones and arbitrate any dispute between the parties. Payments will be released sequentially to Amit Chawla upon milestone approval.
