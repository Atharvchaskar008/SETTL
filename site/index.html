MilestoneVault

MilestoneVault turns a deal written in plain words into a locked agreement. People join, pretend money is locked, and each person is paid only when their stage is approved.

The Problem

Afraid to go first

When work is paid in stages, both sides are afraid to go first.

Holders have hidden rules

Whoever holds the money has hidden rules.

Vague words cause disputes

Agreements use vague words that turn into disputes.

No room for mistakes

A program that holds money cannot afford a mistake.

How It Works

  1. 1
    Describe

    The organiser types the deal in plain language.

  2. 2
    Check

    A deal checker turns it into a checklist and marks unclear lines, which the organiser fixes.

  3. 3
    Join

    Everyone agrees and the payers deposit their share. The deal is locked.

  4. 4
    Do and approve

    The payee marks a stage done. The payers approve and the payee is paid, or a payer disputes and the judge chooses pay or refund.

  5. 5
    Finish

    Every stage is settled and the money adds up exactly.

Payers Payers put money in.
Payees Payees are paid, one per stage.
Judge The judge settles disputes and can never take the money.
